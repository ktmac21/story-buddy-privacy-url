Story Buddy Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 August 2026

Story Buddy makes personalised bedtime stories for children. A grown-up creates the account and sets up each child's explorer profile. This policy explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, and how to get it deleted.

The short version. We collect the parent's email address, the details you give us about each child so their stories can feature them, and the stories themselves. We use AI services to write and illustrate stories. We show no advertising, we use no analytics or tracking SDKs, and we do not sell personal information or share it for advertising.

Who we are

Story Buddy is provided by Kathryn Eagles. If you have questions about this policy or want your data deleted, email us at storybuddy@gmail.com.

What we collect

WhatWhy we have it
Parent's email address and password To create and secure the account. Passwords are stored only as salted hashes by our authentication provider — we never see them.
Child profile details: first name or nickname, age, hair colour, eye colour, pets, and interests To write stories that feature your child and to illustrate them consistently. You choose what to enter; a nickname works as well as a real name.
Stories and illustrations generated in the app, and the topic chosen for each one To show the story, read it aloud, and keep it in your library so it can be re-read.
Usage counts: how many stories were generated in the current month To enforce the monthly allowance for your plan.
Subscription status: your plan, when it last changed, and an identifier linking your account to our payments provider To unlock what you have paid for and to restore it on a new device.

What we do not collect

  • No advertising identifiers, and no advertising of any kind.
  • No analytics, attribution, or tracking SDKs.
  • No location data, contacts, photos, or device address book.
  • No audio recordings. Story Buddy plays narration; it never listens.
  • No payment card details. Card data goes to Apple or Google and never reaches us.

We may also disclose information where the law requires it, or to protect the rights and safety of our users. We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.

Children's privacy

Story Buddy is designed for a grown-up to set up and a child to enjoy. Account creation sits behind a grown-up check, and it is the parent who enters each child's details and manages the subscription. Children are not asked to provide any information themselves, and there is no chat, no social feature, no user-to-user contact, and no advertising anywhere in the app.

The information a parent provides about a child is used solely to generate and illustrate that child's stories, and to show them again in the library. As a parent you can review, change, or delete anything you have entered at any time, and you can withdraw consent by deleting the child's profile or the whole account.

Storage and security

All traffic between the app and our services is encrypted in transit. Database access is restricted per account, so one account cannot read another's children or stories. Story illustrations are held in private storage, filed under the account that created them and readable by no one else — not by another account, and not by anyone with the address. The app opens them through short-lived signed links that stop working after a week.

How long we keep it

We keep your account data for as long as the account exists. Deleting an explorer profile in the app permanently deletes that child's details and their saved stories. Deleting your account removes the account, every child profile on it, and every saved story. We do not retain long-term backup copies of deleted data.

Your choices and rights

  • See or correct your data: profile details are editable in the app; email us for anything you cannot reach.
  • Delete a child's data: Account → Explorer profiles → Remove.
  • Delete your account and everything in it: Account → Delete account. It takes effect immediately. You can also email storybuddy@gmail.com and we will action it within 30 days.
  • Get a copy of your data: email us and we will send an export.
  • Manage your subscription: in your App Store or Play Store account settings. Billing and cancellation are handled by the store, not by us.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under the GDPR, UK GDPR, or state privacy laws such as the CCPA — including the right to object to processing and to complain to your local data protection authority. Where the GDPR applies, our lawful basis is the contract with you for running the service, and consent for the child details you choose to provide.

International transfers

Our providers process data in the United States and the European Union. Where data leaves your region we rely on the providers' standard contractual clauses.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your information, we will update this page and change the date at the top. Significant changes will be notified in the app.
